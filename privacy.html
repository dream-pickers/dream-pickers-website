This notice describes the enquiry form on the Dream Pickers website.

What you provide

The form collects your name, email, phone number, project type, location, optional budget, message, and agreement to be contacted. Please do not include identity documents, payment information, or other sensitive information.

How it is used and stored

Dream Pickers uses these details to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project. Netlify processes and stores form submissions for the studio and applies spam filtering. If the studio enables email notifications, submission details are also delivered to its configured email account. Submission data is not displayed in the public website.

Choices and requests

Submitting an enquiry is optional. To request a correction, deletion, or to withdraw your request for follow-up, reply to the studio's response or use the enquiry form with a clear request. Include enough information for the studio to identify your earlier enquiry.

Other services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and reference photography from Unsplash. Your browser contacts these services to display those resources. Netlify also receives technical request information needed to host the website and protect forms.
